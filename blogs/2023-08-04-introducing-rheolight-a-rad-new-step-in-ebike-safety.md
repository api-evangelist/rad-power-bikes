When we released our new round of limited edition RadRunners, we didn’t want to just give our riders more color options — we wanted to double-down on safety. That’s why we’ve equipped both the Pool Shark Blue RadRunner 2 and the Boss Moss Green RadRunner 3 Plus with RheoLight, a special coating designed to boost night visibility. “With more folks riding electric bikes than ever before, safety has never been more important in the ebike industry,” Sarah Bruce Courtney, Rad’s director of Product Development, told the Scenic Route.
